\section{Data}
\paragraph{Ladders.} Three weekly ladders of named endpoints: Reddit comment karma and submission karma by community (December 2018--December 2022, 212--213 weeks; daily negative karma set to zero before weekly sums) from the Pushshift archive, and Facebook page interactions from CrowdTangle (November 2020--June 2022, 86 weeks, the stable instrument era; days flagged by the day guard excluded). Universes: top $K$ = 12,500 (comments), 5,000 (submissions), 3,500 (pages), with membership penalised for absence; endpoints ever ranked inside $4K$ in a week are tracked so that every top-$k$ set with $k \le K$ is complete. Log activity $x=\log(1+a)$.
\paragraph{Exposure and sealing.} Every contact with these panels is logged in \texttt{llm\_fitting/EXPOSURE\_LEDGER.md}. A 2023--2024 extension of both Reddit ladders and a Wikipedia ladder are sealed; nothing in the paper uses them. The registered tests for them are summarised in Section~\ref{sec:registered}.
\paragraph{Instagram.} A fourth ladder, Instagram accounts, is sampled through a keyword query; its raw panels are not modelled. It enters only through a measured model of its censoring, which is how it contributes to the pooled boundary ratio (Section~\ref{sec:boundary}).

\section{Blocks, cohorts and permanent rank}
Blocks are non-overlapping and aligned to each panel's end (52 weeks; 26 for the boundary test; 13 or 26 on Facebook). A block level requires presence in 90\% of the block's weeks. Permanent rank is a community's mean weekly rank over a fixed block, absent weeks counted at that week's floor. Cohorts are fixed on a block disjoint from the blocks they are measured on. The reason is selection: a community observed high this week is high partly by passing chance, and conditioning on current rank selects on that noise. With weekly passing swings of the size measured here this bias is first-order.

\section{Turnover from churn and slope}\label{sec:turnover}
\subsection{Derivation}
Let the ladder be locally linear in log rank at rank $k$, $x(r) = x_k - S_k \log(r/k)$, so the number of names above level $x_k+u$ is $k e^{-u/S_k}$ and their density at height $u$ above the boundary is $(k/S_k)e^{-u/S_k}$. Over an interval the boundary moves by $\Delta\theta_k$ and a name at height $u$ by $\Delta_i$; it leaves the top $k$ when $\Delta_i < \Delta\theta_k - u$. If $\Delta_i$ does not depend on $u$ within the reach of a year's moves,
\begin{equation}
\frac{\mathbb{E}[\#\text{leaving}]}{k} = \frac{1}{S_k}\int_0^\infty e^{-u/S_k}\,\Pr(\Delta < \Delta\theta_k - u)\,du = \mathbb{E}\!\left[1-e^{-D^+/S_k}\right],\quad D = \Delta\theta_k - \Delta .
\end{equation}
Names of the top $k$ that vanish from the universe (share $v_k$) leave with certainty, giving $f_k = v_k + (1-v_k)\,\mathbb{E}[1-e^{-D^+/S_k}]$. For small moves $f_k \approx \mathbb{E}[D^+]/S_k$.
\subsection{Registered and post hoc versions}
The registered expectation used the small-move form with constant density, $v_k + (1-v_k)\mathbb{E}[D^+]/S_k$; it over-predicted on every ladder (median ratio 1.30, 1.27, 1.45) and failed its expectation (within $[0.8,1.25]$ in 11\%, 39\%, 25\% of cells). The exact form above was derived after that failure. It holds in all 48 cells (ratios 0.82--1.18, median 0.98 on each ladder; rmse 0.018, 0.009, 0.024). Inputs: $S_k$ from a regression of level on log rank over ranks $k/\sqrt2$--$k\sqrt2$ at the start block; $\Delta_i$ for names ranked $k/2$--$k$ present in both blocks.

\section{The balance at every rank}\label{sec:boundary}
\subsection{The rate law}
Names move by Itô processes with local drift $\mu$ and variance $\sigma^2$; the density of levels obeys the forward equation, and integrating over the top $k$ gives $dU_k/dt = \overline{\mu}_{\text{top }k} + \sigma^2(x_k)/(2S_k)$. For the shape $U_k-U_K$ this gives Eq.~3 of the main text. The stationary form is $S_k = \sigma_k^2/(2\bar s_k)$, Fernholz's gap identity in continuum notation; the arithmetic-share analogue is Gomez's displacement term $\tfrac12(\zeta-1)\nu^2$ with $\zeta = 1/S$.
\subsection{Estimators}
\emph{Lasting variance}: for a cohort and three blocks $a<b<c$, $[\mathrm{var}(x_c-x_a) - \tfrac12(\mathrm{var}(x_b-x_a)+\mathrm{var}(x_c-x_b))]/(b-a)$, which cancels passing swings and the block-averaging offset; a variogram estimator agrees within about 10\%. \emph{Crossings}: the reordering gain of the top $k$ (mean of the top $k$ at the end minus the end-mean of the initial members), differenced between spacings of two and four half-years; the difference removes passing swings and equals the lasting crossing gain plus a composition term $C$, which is $+25$ to $+35$\% of the statistic on Reddit and $-30$\% on Facebook. \emph{Slide}: the mean change of a cohort's members above $k$ against the mean of the universe.
\subsection{Results}
Pooled ratio of measured to predicted (geometric mean over $K/16\ldots K$): comments 1.069 (1.011--1.127), submissions 0.802 (0.680--1.004), Facebook 0.977 (0.802--1.202), Instagram 0.995 (0.852--1.213); four ladders 0.955 (0.889--1.054). With one Poisson weight per community shared by the variance and every start, and the starts resampled (Section 4 of the run record), the intervals are comments 0.986--1.152, submissions 0.633--1.009, Facebook 0.840--1.119, and three ladders 0.942 (0.855--1.040). A year ahead from earlier variance: comments 0.944, submissions 0.617. Calibrated rivals: jumps to random ranks 1.56; fixed-ladder log-rank diffusion 1.05. The submissions shortfall at ranks 300--600 (0.61--0.72) is unexplained; on simulations of the generative model with known lasting variance the ratio is structured by cut-off (0.68--0.97 at $k$ = 625--1,250; 1.16--1.30 at 2,500--5,000), so part of it may be the estimator's interaction with the size law.

\section{The generative model}
\subsection{Specification}
Observed log activity $x_i = L_i + m_i + \varepsilon_i$ with $\varepsilon$ observation noise and $m$ an AR(1) passing swing with $t$-tails. In the implementation (\texttt{rankdiff\_rdm.py}, \texttt{lasting="movinghome"}) the slow component follows the earlier permanent--transitory model's mean reversion toward a home $H_i$ that itself random-walks: $L_{t+1} = L_t - \kappa(L_t-H_t) + \epsilon_t$, $H_{t+1} = H_t + g(r(H_t)) + \eta_t$, $\mathrm{Var}\,\eta = \sigma_L^2 = \sigma_0^2 e^{-2\beta(H-L_{\rm ref})}$. The main text's lasting level is $H$; the reverting part of $L$ is the medium swing. Drift by rank $g$ is either the measured slide profile or the fixed point that holds the end-of-training ladder.
\subsection{The variance error and its correction}
With $\mathrm{Var}\,\epsilon = q$, the one-step variance of $L$ is $[2q+\kappa\sigma_L^2]/(2-\kappa)$. The frozen form set $q = \sigma_p^2-\sigma_L^2$, which is the earlier model's one-step variance minus $\sigma_L^2$; preserving it requires $q=\sigma_p^2-\kappa\sigma_L^2/2$ (\texttt{lasting="movinghome\_vp"}). A known-answer test confirms both statements.

\section{Persistence}
\subsection{The averaging null}
For a random walk with increment variance $v$ averaged over blocks of $B$ steps, consecutive block-mean changes have variance $v(2B^2+1)/(3B)$ and covariance $v(B^2-1)/(6B)$, so correlation $(B^2-1)/(2(2B^2+1)) \to 1/4$ ($0.2499$ at $B=52$; Spearman $0.239$) \cite{working1960}.
\subsection{The gapped statistic}
Changes between windows of $w$ weeks $h$ weeks apart, the second change starting $g$ weeks after the first ends; any independent-increment process plus white noise gives zero; a short-memory swing gives a small negative value. Synthetic check (5 seeds): random walk + noise + OU, $-0.032$, $-0.009$, $-0.005$ at gaps 0, 13, 26; with drift by community (sd 0.4 a year), $+0.17$ to $+0.19$.

\section{Gomez's decomposition of the arithmetic shares}
For consecutive 52-week blocks, with $P_a$ the top $k$ by total activity at $a$, $W$ the top-$k$ total and $D$ the top-$K$ total, the relative change of the share is exactly
$\frac{s_b}{s_a}-1 = \big[(\sum_{P_a} t_b/W_a - D_b/D_a) + (W_b - \sum_{P_a} t_b)/W_a\big]/(D_b/D_a)$: a within term (the initial members against the total) and a between (displacement) term.

The main text's test therefore uses the mean-log form, permanent-rank cohorts and spacing differences.

\section{Null worlds}\label{sec:null}
Every headline statistic computed by the same code in four simulated worlds (24,000 names, $K$ = 5,000, 212 weeks): (i) size-blind random growth (lasting $\sigma$ = 0.10 a week, no drift); (ii) firm-like growth ($\sigma \propto e^{-0.2(L-L_K)}$, common drift $-0.005$, reflecting floor); (iii) neutral copying (1.5 million items a week drawn from last week's distribution, innovation 0.003); (iv) the generative model calibrated to Reddit submissions. Worlds (i)--(ii) start from a Zipf ladder with noise and carry the data-like passing swings.

\section{Rank change and set replacement}
Iñiguez et al.'s rank change $C(R)$ is the probability that the element at rank $R$ differs between consecutive observations. Weekly $C(R)$ on the data: Reddit submissions 0.07, 0.36, 0.79, 0.99 at $R$ = 1, 3, 10, 100; comments 0.03, 0.46, 0.87, 0.99; Facebook 0.52, 0.74, 0.94, 1.00. The top is stable in rank order on every ladder while the top set's membership turns over fastest; both follow from a ladder whose neighbour gaps are about $S/R$ and whose slope $S$ is smallest at the head.

\section{Registration, post hoc analyses and test counts}\label{sec:registered}
Expected outcomes were committed to the repository before each analysis (\texttt{runs/2026-10-18\_pnas\_push/PLAN.md}), and each is scored in \texttt{RESULTS.md}, failures included. Of the expectations for this paper's analyses, those that failed or held only partly include: the linear-density turnover formula (A1-1); the size of the two-year counterfactual changes (A3-2, A3-3); item-count scaling on Facebook (A7-4); the first frozen model's long-panel gate (A5-1, A5-2) and its corrected form's 13-week median (A5b-2); the 2$\times$2 spread on comments (A5c, by 0.0005); out-of-period retention against carrying forward on submissions (A6-2); the joint interval's widening on submissions (A9-2); and the same-side conditional means on submissions (D-2). Post hoc analyses: the exact turnover formula; the averaging-aware persistence statistic; the corrected variance bookkeeping; the general Gomez formula after the misapplied Pareto form. The gate ledger lists 83 evaluations of the movement test across panels and specifications since 2026-10-11. Tests on sealed 2023--2024 Reddit data are written into a protocol (primaries: the boundary ratio in period and out of time, the blackout of June 2023, retention against a logistic baseline, persistence, the size exponent and the model's held-out movement) that is to be adopted before those data are opened; it was not adopted at writing.
