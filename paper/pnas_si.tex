\section{Data}
\paragraph{Ladders.} Three weekly ladders of named endpoints: Reddit comment karma and submission karma by community (December 2018--December 2022, 212--213 weeks; daily negative karma set to zero before weekly sums) from the Pushshift archive, and Facebook page interactions from CrowdTangle (November 2020--June 2022, 86 weeks, the stable instrument era; days flagged by the day guard excluded). Universes: top $K$ = 12,500 (comments), 5,000 (submissions), 3,500 (pages), with membership penalised for absence; endpoints ever ranked inside $4K$ in a week are tracked so that every top-$k$ set with $k \le K$ is complete. Log activity $x=\log(1+a)$.
\paragraph{Terms of use.} Reddit data come from the Pushshift archive; the archive and the community-level panels built from it are not redistributed, while analyses may name communities. CrowdTangle's terms for researchers allowed insights from its data to be published but not the raw data, and allowed the IDs of the Pages studied to be posted so that others could rebuild the list; we follow that line, as careful CrowdTangle studies did (no per-Page series are released). Details and sources are in the repository (\url{llm_fitting/runs/2026-10-18_pnas_push/DATA_AVAILABILITY_NOTES.md}).
\paragraph{Exposure and sealing.} Every contact with these panels is logged in \texttt{llm\_fitting/EXPOSURE\_LEDGER.md}. A 2023--2024 extension of both Reddit ladders and a Wikipedia ladder are sealed; nothing in the paper uses them. The proposed tests for them (a draft protocol, not adopted) are summarised in Section~\ref{sec:registered}.
\paragraph{Instagram.} A fourth ladder, Instagram accounts, is sampled through a keyword query; its raw panels are not modelled. It enters only through a measured model of its censoring, which is how it contributes to the pooled boundary ratio (Section~\ref{sec:boundary}).

\section{Blocks, cohorts and permanent rank}
Blocks are non-overlapping and aligned to each panel's end (52 weeks; 26 for the boundary test; 13 or 26 on Facebook). A block level requires presence in 90\% of the block's weeks. Permanent rank is a community's mean weekly rank over a fixed block, absent weeks counted at that week's floor. Cohorts are fixed on a block disjoint from the blocks they are measured on. The reason is selection: a community observed high this week is high partly by passing chance, and conditioning on current rank selects on that noise. With weekly passing swings of the size measured here this bias is first-order.

\section{Turnover from churn and slope}\label{sec:turnover}
\subsection{Derivation}
Let the ladder be locally linear in log rank at rank $k$, $x(r) = x_k - S_k \log(r/k)$, so the number of names above level $x_k+u$ is $k e^{-u/S_k}$ and their density at height $u$ above the boundary is $(k/S_k)e^{-u/S_k}$. Over an interval the boundary moves by $\Delta\theta_k$ and a name at height $u$ by $\Delta_i$; it leaves the top $k$ when $\Delta_i < \Delta\theta_k - u$. If $\Delta_i$ does not depend on $u$ within the reach of a year's moves,
\begin{equation}
\frac{\mathbb{E}[\#\text{leaving}]}{k} = \frac{1}{S_k}\int_0^\infty e^{-u/S_k}\,\Pr(\Delta < \Delta\theta_k - u)\,du = \mathbb{E}\!\left[1-e^{-D^+/S_k}\right],\quad D = \Delta\theta_k - \Delta .
\end{equation}
Names of the top $k$ that vanish from the universe (share $v_k$) leave with certainty, giving
\begin{equation}
f_k \;=\; v_k + (1-v_k)\,\mathbb{E}\!\left[1-e^{-D^+/S_k}\right].
\label{eq:S_turnover}
\end{equation}
For small moves $f_k \approx \mathbb{E}[D^+]/S_k$. The typical downward move at the boundary is nearly constant across scale (0.21--0.40 log units), while the slope rises from 0.7--1.0 at the head to 1.2--2.3 lower down, so the top sets turn over fastest.
\subsection{Registered and post hoc versions}
The registered expectation used the small-move form with constant density, $v_k + (1-v_k)\mathbb{E}[D^+]/S_k$; it over-predicted on every ladder (median ratio 1.30, 1.27, 1.45) and failed its expectation (within $[0.8,1.25]$ in 11\%, 39\%, 25\% of cells). The exact form above was derived after that failure. It holds in all 48 cells (ratios 0.82--1.18, median 0.98 on each ladder; rmse 0.018, 0.009, 0.024). Inputs: $S_k$ from a regression of level on log rank over ranks $k/\sqrt2$--$k\sqrt2$ at the start block; $\Delta_i$ for names ranked $k/2$--$k$ present in both blocks.

\subsection{Turnover profiles and geometry}
Studies of cultural evolution summarize how the number of new entries $z$ into a top-$y$ list grows with $y$ as $z = a y^b$, and read departures of $b$ from its neutral value as evidence of biased copying \cite{bentley2007,acerbi2014,evans2011}. With $z = f_y\, y$, the data give $b$ = 0.95 (submissions, $y$ = 78--2,496) and 0.92 (comments, 195--6,240). Under identical calibrated dynamics, the data's ladder gives $b$ = 0.88 (size-blind) and 0.91 (firm-like), and a ladder of constant slope 0.99 and 1.04 (20 runs each, $y$ = 78--2,500). The ladder alone shifts $b$ by 0.11--0.13, so an inference about copying from a turnover profile needs a control for the ladder's slope (post hoc, PLAN.md addendum R13; R13-3 passed).

\section{The balance at every rank}\label{sec:boundary}
\subsection{The rate law}
Names move by Itô processes with local drift $\mu$ and variance $\sigma^2$; the density of levels obeys the forward equation, and integrating over the top $k$ gives $dU_k/dt = \overline{\mu}_{\text{top }k} + \sigma^2(x_k)/(2S_k)$. For the shape $U_k-U_K$ this gives Eq.~2 of the main text. The stationary form is $S_k = \sigma_k^2/(2(\bar s_k - e_k))$, Fernholz's gap identity in continuum notation and, with one drift, the Atlas slope $\sigma^2/(2g)$ \cite{fernholz2002,fernholz2017,fernholzfernholz2020}; the arithmetic-share analogue is Gomez's displacement term, $\tfrac12\nu^2(a_k/\bar a_{\rm top})/S_k$ in general and $\tfrac12(\zeta-1)\nu^2$ on a Pareto tail with index $\zeta = 1/S > 1$ \cite{gomez2023}. Because $c_k = \sigma_k^2/(2S_k)$, the ratio of crossing gain to slide is $c_k/\bar s_k = S^\ast_k/S_k$: crossings fall short of the slide exactly when the ladder is steeper than its own stationary steepness.
\subsection{Estimators}
\emph{Lasting variance}: for a cohort and three blocks $a<b<c$, $[\mathrm{var}(x_c-x_a) - \tfrac12(\mathrm{var}(x_b-x_a)+\mathrm{var}(x_c-x_b))]/(b-a)$, which cancels passing swings and the block-averaging offset; a variogram estimator agrees within about 10\%. \emph{Crossings}: the reordering gain of the top $k$ (mean of the top $k$ at the end minus the end-mean of the initial members), differenced between spacings of two and four half-years; the difference removes passing swings and equals the lasting crossing gain plus a composition term $C$, which is $+25$ to $+35$\% of the statistic on Reddit and $-30$\% on Facebook. \emph{Slide}: the mean change of a cohort's members above $k$ against the mean of the universe.
\subsection{Results}
Pooled ratio of measured to predicted (geometric mean over $K/16\ldots K$): comments 1.069 (1.011--1.127), submissions 0.802 (0.680--1.004), Facebook 0.977 (0.802--1.202), Instagram 0.995 (0.852--1.213); four ladders 0.955 (0.889--1.054). A year ahead from earlier variance: comments 0.944, submissions 0.617. Calibrated rivals: jumps to random ranks 1.56; fixed-ladder log-rank diffusion 1.05. The submissions shortfall at ranks 300--600 (0.61--0.72) is unexplained; on simulations of the generative model with known lasting variance the ratio is structured by cut-off (0.68--0.97 at $k$ = 625--1,250; 1.16--1.30 at 2,500--5,000), so part of it may be the estimator's interaction with the size law.

\subsection{Time scales}\label{sec:timescales}
Three time scales follow from the measured lasting variance $\sigma_k^2$, slope $S_k$ and balanced slope $S^\ast_k$. (i) Lasting movement crosses one e-fold of rank in $S_k^2/\sigma_k^2$; a member placed uniformly in log rank within a top set lies on average one e-fold above its boundary, so this is the typical time for a member's lasting movement to reach the boundary. (ii) For a ladder of uniform slope between $k$ and $K$, linearized about $S^\ast$, the shape relaxes toward its balance with time constant $\tau_k = 2S^{\ast2}_k\ln(K/k)/\sigma_k^2$. (iii) In the reflecting-barrier model of random growth, the cross-section converges at rate $\lambda = \sigma^2/(8S^2)$ \cite{gllm2016}; at balance, with $S = S^\ast$ and $\ln(K/k)\approx 4$, (ii) and (iii) nearly coincide, and the ratio of the shape's time scale to the movement time is about $8(S^\ast/S)^2$.

These formulas apply to a single slope and a homogeneous drift; the ladders are curved, the drift is by rank, and exits are omitted. Exits shorten the convergence time of (iii), little at the head, where they are rare. In rank-based models without entry and exit the mean-field equation need not have a spectral gap at all \cite{bakersmyth2026}. The numbers are therefore orders of magnitude, and model-implied: they describe how the measured ingredients act if held fixed, not forecasts.

\section{The generative model}
\subsection{Specification}
Observed log activity $x_i = L_i + m_i + \varepsilon_i$ with $\varepsilon$ observation noise and $m$ an AR(1) passing swing with $t$-tails. In the implementation (\texttt{rankdiff\_rdm.py}, \texttt{lasting="movinghome"}) the slow component follows the earlier permanent--transitory model's mean reversion toward a home $H_i$ that itself random-walks: $L_{t+1} = L_t - \kappa(L_t-H_t) + \epsilon_t$, $H_{t+1} = H_t + g(r(H_t)) + \eta_t$, $\mathrm{Var}\,\eta = \sigma_L^2 = \sigma_0^2 e^{-2\beta(H-L_{\rm ref})}$. The main text's lasting level is $H$; the reverting part of $L$ is the medium swing. Drift by rank $g$ is either the measured slide profile or the fixed point that holds the end-of-training ladder.
\subsection{The variance error and its correction}
With $\mathrm{Var}\,\epsilon = q$, the one-step variance of $L$ is $[2q+\kappa\sigma_L^2]/(2-\kappa)$. The frozen form set $q = \sigma_p^2-\sigma_L^2$, which is the earlier model's one-step variance minus $\sigma_L^2$; preserving it requires $q=\sigma_p^2-\kappa\sigma_L^2/2$ (\texttt{lasting="movinghome\_vp"}). A known-answer test confirms both statements.

\section{Persistence}
\subsection{The averaging null}
For a random walk with increment variance $v$ averaged over blocks of $B$ steps, consecutive block-mean changes have variance $v(2B^2+1)/(3B)$ and covariance $v(B^2-1)/(6B)$, so correlation $(B^2-1)/(2(2B^2+1)) \to 1/4$ ($0.2499$ at $B=52$; Spearman $0.239$) \cite{working1960}.
\subsection{The gapped statistic}
Changes between windows of $w$ weeks $h$ weeks apart, the second change starting $g$ weeks after the first ends; any independent-increment process plus white noise gives zero; a short-memory swing gives a small negative value. Synthetic check (5 seeds): random walk + noise + OU, $-0.032$, $-0.009$, $-0.005$ at gaps 0, 13, 26; with drift by community (sd 0.4 a year), $+0.17$ to $+0.19$.

\section{Model atlas: every simulated model and how each fails}\label{sec:null}
\paragraph{Selection rule.} The models represent the standard explanations of a stationary hierarchy with churning members: random growth with a size-blind and with a firm-like volatility law (Gibrat; the firm-growth literature), neutral copying (cultural evolution), and the generative model built here, plus, from round 6, three standard forms of heterogeneous growth (Section~\ref{sec:hetero}). Each growth law is run from a ladder of constant slope and from the data's 2019 ladder, so that geometry and dynamics can be separated. Every model is reported in full on every statistic, including those it fails; none is dropped after scoring. Models that were fitted and replaced during development (earlier stacks, an ablation ladder, drift by name, life-cycle drift, jumps to random ranks) are listed in the repository's gate ledger and record; jumps to random ranks enter the boundary comparison (1.56).

\section{Heterogeneous growth}\label{sec:hetero}
\paragraph{Specification.} Three standard forms add a community-specific term $a_{it}$ to the lasting level of the calibrated model, $\Delta H_{it} = g(r_{it}) + a_{it} + \sigma_0 e^{-\beta(H_{it}-H_0)}\eta_{it}$: (a) a fixed Gaussian effect $b_i \sim N(0,\sigma_b^2)$, drawn once per community, as in heterogeneous income profiles and second-order rank models \cite{guvenen2009,guvenen2021,fik2013}; (b) an autoregressive growth rate, $u_{i,t+1} = \rho u_{it} + \sigma_u\sqrt{1-\rho^2}\,\xi_{it}$, as with Ornstein--Uhlenbeck fitness in ecology and autoregressive growth components of firms \cite{mallmin2026,sterk2021,gllm2016}; and (c) exposure to the platform's weekly total, $(\lambda_i - \bar\lambda_K) f_t$, with $f_t$ the observed weekly change of mean log activity over the tracked universe, leaving out the community itself, and $\lambda_i$ each community's loading on it, estimated on the training weeks, with the spread of loadings corrected for estimation noise, as with factor loadings in finance \cite{carhart1997,ichiba2011}. A world driven by the observed $f_t$ is a conditional historical world, not a forecast of platform activity. The drift by rank is re-solved with the heterogeneity present, so that it does not count the composition twice.
\paragraph{Calibration.} Forms (a) and (b) are calibrated by equally weighted minimum distance \cite{abowd1989,altonji1996,hryshko2012} on weekly growth autocovariances at lags 1--8, 13, 26, 39 and 52 (the long lags are covariances of changes that share no weeks; curvature of the variance profile is not used, since rank-dependent volatility can mimic it), in three strata of permanent rank, each scaled by its bootstrap standard deviation, with simulated moments computed through the same universe and selection as the data. Form (c) has no free parameter. No form is calibrated on the statistics it is tested on: the gapped correlations, the prior-decliner contrast, head shares, turnover, retention and the boundary ratio.
\paragraph{Recovery.} Under heterogeneous growth the variance of $h$-week changes grows as $qh + Bh^2$, so the two-spacing estimator of lasting variance reads $q + 3Bh$ and the size exponent is attenuated \cite{guvenen2009,hryshko2012}. The estimators are therefore rerun on panels simulated from each calibrated heterogeneous world, with known truth (20 panels each).
