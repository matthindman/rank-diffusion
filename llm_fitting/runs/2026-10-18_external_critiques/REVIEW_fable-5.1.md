# Independent review of the rank-diffusion PNAS paper

_Read-only review, 2026-10-18. Numbers are recomputed from the saved JSONs under `llm_fitting/runs/` where possible; record sections are cited as §. No file was created or changed. Note: during this session another process modified `llm_fitting/mts_boundary_law.py`, the boundary JSON cells, `EXPOSURE_LEDGER.md` and added `runs/2026-10-18_pooled_ratio/`; the point estimates I recomputed from those cells match the committed record exactly, and I quote the new pooled numbers as uncommitted._

## 1. Verdict

As it stands PNAS would not send this out with enthusiasm: it is two half-papers (A: a permanent–transitory movement model whose structural implication failed; B: a rank-by-rank crossing balance whose mathematics is stochastic portfolio theory's), every result is in-sample on development data, and the forecasting arm fails by its own registered scores. The single largest gap is not out-of-sample confirmation but the absence of one dynamical picture: A maintains the ladder by reversion to a personal home (MODEL_STATUS §1), while the program's own measurements (§2z-ay "the home pulls endpoints back, which the data contradicts"; §2z-bu item 3 "no return toward own level") and B's whole apparatus say the ladder is held by drift-by-rank, crossings and entry, so the two lines currently contradict each other on the mechanism the paper is about. The one change that would most raise the odds is to make B the paper's spine, demote A to the measurement of individual movement that feeds B (the lasting variance by rank and the lock-in forecaster), and show in one figure that A's permanent-innovation variance, B's two cohort estimators and the crossing-implied variance 2SR̃ are the same quantity by rank. With that unification, the capture-delay audit below, and the sealed Reddit bridge run as registered in §2z-cp, this becomes a paper referees cannot easily sink; without them it reads as a careful, honest, over-long technical report.

## 2. The ambitious version

**What the data can carry.** The exact decomposition (`balance_accounting.topk_accounting`, §2z-cr) is the paper's best object: for any top set, shape change = members' movement + reordering + entry, and the reordering term is predicted from two independently measured things. On Reddit comments that prediction holds at seven cut-offs by two estimators (§2z-cm, §2z-co), and the same half-year decomposition shows crossings returning +0.076 to +0.111 log units in all 18 cells while members' movement swings from −0.29 to +0.07 (`runs/2026-10-15_comments_2021_head/output_comments.txt`). That is a measured, falsifiable statement of what holds the shape of online attention. The reasonable headline is: **"Attention inequality is held in place by a measured balance: crossings at every rank return what incumbents lose; platform design moves the ladder only through incumbents' drift by rank, and the ladder then relaxes over years toward the shape current movement sustains."**

**The unreasonable target, argued for.** Claim that *the rank-size curve of attention is a quotient, not a law*: S_k = σ_k²/(2·net slide_k), so the curve's universal steepening in the tail is the rank profile of volatility (0.007 → 0.013 a week on comments, `fig_why_it_curves.png`) over the profile of slide-plus-entry, and that a platform's design sets the slide profile while the volatility profile is a size-scaling law. The repository already has the pieces: §2z-bf (A9) found σ²/S close to constant across ranks; §2z-cd/§2z-cv built S* by rank; §2z-cc measured entry. What would carry it: (i) the stationary form S* against observed S by rank on all four ladders, not only Reddit (needs the entry term on `facebook_a` and `instagram_hm`; code exists in `mts_balance_law.py` and `mts_replenish.py`); (ii) the firm-growth size–variance scaling (Stanley et al. 1996; Riccaboni et al. 2008 PNAS) estimated within community on the dailies, which would turn "volatility falls with size: type or state?" (§2z-cj, §2z-ck) into a measured exponent; (iii) the sealed Wikipedia panel as the long-span test. If S* ≈ S holds on comments, Facebook and Instagram and fails only on submissions after a dated design change, the paper has a law of structure with one documented out-of-equilibrium case and a relaxation time, which is exactly what Gabaix–Lasry–Lions–Moll asked for and never had on a platform with a dated intervention.

**How A and B should relate.** Keep from A what transported (§6 addendum 2026-07-12): the movement moments (E2, 0.032 vs 0.171), the one-amplitude factorization, the daily-replication noise floor. Drop A's personal-home maintenance mechanism; B replaces it. Make A's σ_perm(z) the microfoundation of B's σ_k² and verify they agree. Treat A's "upper movement tails under-dispersed" and B's lock-in under-forecast as the same residual (two-speed movement, §2z-cz) and build the registered jump-aware forecaster. The headline figure set: Fig 2 (decomposition, all four ladders, not just Reddit), Fig 3 (crossings predicted on four ladders, presented as variance-by-rank from four estimators), Fig 5 (June 2021: drift fell within days, head crossings 2.4 → 7.8 per 1,000 weeks, concentration of the top 78 falling 51% → 35% toward a sustained 16% with τ 2–6 years), Fig 6 (lock-in with the two-speed correction, registered on the bridge).

## 3. The short seller's case

**(1) "Crossings = σ²/2S holds rank by rank on four ladders."** Evidence against: the crossings are the smooth side on every panel and the cohort variance is the erratic side. On submissions the variance strata read 0.0033 (n 145), 0.0141 (n 279), 0.0126 (n 568), 0.0088 (n 1,123), 0.0144 a week (`cells/boundary__reddit_submissions_long.json`), while the crossing-implied variance 2SR̃ rises smoothly 0.0073 → 0.0133. With 13-week blocks on comments (`boundary__reddit_comments_ext__block13__K12500.json`) the strata at ranks 390–1,562 give 0.0052 and 0.0049 against 0.0081 and 0.0095 with 26-week blocks, and the ratios run 0.72, 1.15, **1.41**, 0.86, 0.91, 1.07, 1.14: two of seven outside the band, not one as §2z-cu's prose says. The two estimators disagree by 12–24% on comments and 20–49% on submissions (§2z-co), the same size as the deviations being tested; the uncommitted pooled computation (`runs/2026-10-18_pooled_ratio/output.txt`) gives comments 1.069 (1.011–1.127) by the variance route, i.e. a significant deviation from 1 in one direction, where the variogram would give one in the other. Submissions fails the registered rule in every form (B1, V3, R2). Facebook's 0.77–1.17 is the K/16…K range; K/64 is 0.71. Instagram's K/64 is 2.49, plotted as a filled symbol in `fig_four_ladders.png` because the "<150 names" rule is applied to k, not to the 47–102-member strata behind it. Hostile phrasing: "A band 1.77 wide, a rule of five of seven, a noisy denominator, and the one panel that fails is reported as 'holds in the body'." Fix: present the test as four variance estimates by rank (variance route, variogram, A's σ_perm, 2SR̃) with dependence-aware intervals, state the power from planted worlds, and say plainly which ladders pass the registered rule (comments, Facebook K/16…K, Instagram K/16…K) and which does not.

**(2) "The agreement is not automatic."** The rivals that fail (§2z-cv) are rank-space toys: 1%-a-week random displacement and neighbour swaps. Every level-diffusion variant passes (0.85–1.25, §2z-cq), including fixed drift types, jumps and t tails; only still-sorting volatility types move the ratio, and no world calibrated to the data was run (V5 outstanding). Hostile phrasing: "The test establishes that log-attention diffuses in levels, which Gabaix (1999) and Blumm et al. (2012) already assume; it cannot tell apart any of the models anyone would fit." Fix: say what the test excludes and what it does not; run V5; and make the structural claim (S* ≈ S) carry the weight instead.

**(3) "Ex ante lock-in forecasts discriminate who stays, at par with a logistic fitted to outcomes."** From `lockin__*.json`: Brier model / constant turnover / logistic = 0.1252 / 0.1578 / 0.1244 (comments), 0.1357 / 0.1660 / 0.1309 (submissions), 0.1607 / 0.1855 / 0.1587 (Facebook), 0.1621 / 0.2017 / 0.1615 (Instagram). The logistic has three parameters (intercept, log rank, log horizon) and no movement information, and it beats the model on all four ladders. In aggregate at the first horizon the model is biased low everywhere (comments 0.847 forecast against 0.872 observed; submissions 0.817 against 0.868; Facebook 0.756 against 0.797), while constant turnover is within 0.01 at every ladder, and at h ≥ 3 on comments the logistic's aggregate (0.754, 0.717, 0.689) tracks observed (0.750, 0.711, 0.676) where the model gives 0.716, 0.666, 0.605. T3 failed. Hostile phrasing: "Discrimination here means 'rank 5 stays in the top 100 more often than rank 95'; the only thing genuinely ex ante is the level, and it is wrong in the same direction on every ladder." Fix: present the under-forecast as the finding (incumbents are stickier than diffusion implies), register the empirical-shape or jump forecaster before any new outcome, and add a baseline that uses gap and own volatility so the comparison is fair.

**(4) "Crossings are steady, so the shape moves only when drift changes."** On comments, yes (0.076–0.111). On submissions the one-spacing reordering term ranges 0.038–0.118 (§2z-cr), a factor of three, and both terms carry the one-time constants the design removes elsewhere. The statement is also close to a restatement: shape change is defined as the sum of the two terms, so if one is constant the other carries all variation. The content is that σ² and S are nearly stationary while drift by rank is not. Hostile phrasing: "Post hoc, in sample, one ladder, and partly definitional." Fix: report the lasting (two-spacing) decomposition per half-year, on all four ladders, and phrase the claim as a measurement of stationarity of volatility against non-stationarity of drift.

**(5) "The platform change acted through drift and mobility; concentration is heading to 16%."** The tripling of head crossings rests on k = 78 with strata of 31–78 communities: 2.35 (0.80–3.79) → 7.82 (3.75–13.69) per 1,000 weeks (`by_start_post_hoc_output.txt`), unregistered. The 16% sustained share is an index of geometric-mean activity (§2z-cw says so; `PAPER_FRAME_DRAFT_2026-10-15.md` abstract does not), computed with the erratic head variances (4.2–5.1 per 1,000 weeks at K/32–K/16 where 2SR̃ gives 7.3–7.5); substituting the crossing-implied variance raises S* at those ranks by roughly 50–75% and the sustained share with it. There is no control, and the submissions head was already sinking (§2z-cd). Hostile phrasing: "A dated event, a trend that predates it, intervals that span a factor of four, and a counterfactual number that moves with the choice of variance estimator." Fix: state the daily-resolution onset (§2z-ca) as the causal evidence, give the mobility change with its interval, compute the sustained share with both variances, and use comments (whose head moved the other way the same half-year) as the within-platform contrast.

## 4. Fair criticisms of the models

**Balance law and estimators.** The derivation (`BALANCE_LAW_THEORY_NOTE_2026-10-14.md` §2) needs the density smooth on the scale of a step; 52-week steps are 0.7 log units against slopes of 0.9–1.0 at the head, so the small-step condition fails exactly where the head strata are also small. R is not additive over sub-intervals (the `balance_accounting.py` docstring says so), so the spacing difference (R(104)−R(52))/52 is a difference quotient of a concave function, calibrated only by planted exponential ladders at one σ², one S. The variance is measured on one cohort (block 0) for all starts; crossings pooled over 2020–2022 include the post-June-2021 rise in variance (1.36–1.55× on submissions, §2z-co). The slope is a regression over (k/√2, k√2] in log rank; the tests assume n(x_k) = k/S_k, exact only for exponential gaps. Bootstraps treat communities as independent and the three starts share half their weeks. The most load-bearing and least tested assumption: that the boundary occupants' lasting variance equals the permanent-rank cohort's, which is what the type-sorting world violates and what V5 was to test.

**Permanent–transitory model (A).** The personal-home OU is contradicted by the program's own measurements (§2z-ay, §2z-bu); the stationary head it generates is too concentrated (E5 fired); s and κ did not transport. What survives is a measurement model of movement, not a model of structure. Its κ of 0.02–0.04 a week (half-lives of months) sits uneasily with B's "lasting" variance measured at 52–104 weeks as if from a random walk; one of the two descriptions of the permanent component is wrong at the one-year scale, and the paper has to say which.

**Lock-in forecaster.** It stratifies by current rank at a−2 (docstring of `mts_lockin.py`), against the program's own rule of estimating by permanent rank; the Gaussian Φ with variance linear in h is the assumption the data reject (§2z-cz); origins are 3–5 and overlapping; the per-cell counts are not independent. The "ex ante" label is weak: the strata, horizons and functional form were chosen after a year of work on these panels.

**Instagram censoring.** The apparatus (§2z-c) is the best-measured part of the repository. Its weak points: persistent q_i cancels only if the "a"-match share does not drift, which the design admits is unidentified and bounds at 23–41% of displacement variance; the planted worlds assume q independent of level and of volatility, while caption style plausibly covaries with account type; spacings of 14–28 weeks mean the tested quantity is displacement variance, not lasting variance; one start, one year; the head is unmeasurable. The claim should be stated as "the ratio test is robust to the measured censoring layers", not "Instagram obeys the law".

## 5. What we are not seeing

**The dangerous alternative explanation: Pushshift capture.** `runs/2026-10-13_external_evidence/REPORT.md` D4 documents that the monthly files were re-collected with delays ranging from hours to 399 days, in a sawtooth aligned to the collection schedule, with a regime change at 1 July 2021 (about 19 days before, about 360 after). The record checked score truncation (scores at 0/1 do not jump) and the onset date (§2z-ca, 23–24 June). It has not checked differential loss: items removed by moderators or deleted by users between creation and re-collection are missing from late-collected months, and removal rates rise steeply with community size. That mechanism produces a drift by rank, against the head, that follows the collection calendar. Nothing in the record excludes it, and §2z-cd item 6 lists capture delay as unmeasured on the project's own files. This is the first thing a Reddit-data referee will raise.

**Blind spots.** The paper never uses the two Reddit ladders jointly: the same communities' submission karma fell while their comment karma rose in April–September 2021 (§2z-cr), which is a within-community mechanism signature of the feed change (votes per post, §2z-ca) available without names. Entry on Facebook is entry into a tracked CrowdTangle panel, not into the platform. The variance-by-rank curves are one measurement per panel drawn as two years (`fig_why_it_curves.png`). B's claims have never been through the program's own adoption criterion, the rolling-origin movement gate; by the record's own tiers everything in B is "exploratory".

**Literatures missed.** Gomez (Econometrica 2023, "Decomposing the Growth of Top Wealth Shares") decomposes a top share's growth into a within term and a displacement term equal to half the growth variance times the density at the threshold, applied to Forbes 400 and SCF data; this is the balance law in the inequality literature and is absent from `NOVELTY_SEARCH.md` (the only "Gomez" hit in the repository is a different author). Ichiba, Papathanakos, Banner, Karatzas & Fernholz (AAP 2011, hybrid Atlas models: drifts by name and by rank) is the formal frame for the A-versus-B question. Jones & Kim (JPE 2018) derive top inequality from incumbent growth against displacement by entrants, which is the two-speed picture. Batty (Nature 2006, "Rank clocks") documented stable rank-size with churning membership for cities and firms; the repository's own `lit_review/.../rank_dynamics_model_families.md` has it, yet `paper/pnas_outline.tex` (intro par. 1) plans to claim the joint regularity "has not been documented as a single empirical object". Duranton (AER 2007) models city rank churn by innovation jumps. Stanley et al. (1996) and Riccaboni et al. (PNAS 2008) on size–variance scaling bear directly on §2z-cj. Azaele et al. (RMP 2016) on neutral ecology is the same fact for species. Kopczuk, Saez & Song (QJE 2010) is the natural comparison for "lock-in at the top". Salganik, Dodds & Watts (2006) is the framing for inequality with unpredictable displacement.

**Data features ignored.** Post counts versus karma per post on Reddit (done for Instagram only); subreddit creation dates for the age hypothesis (joinable by id); the comments–submissions join; the dated events in REPORT.md §E (the 29 June 2020 ban wave, WallStreetBets January 2021, the February 2021 NSFW change) as checks on exits and the comments head.

**Mechanisms that explain the same patterns differently.** Differential deletion (above); heavier moderation at large communities after the 2020–2021 policy changes; the Home feed personalisation acting on exposure of large communities' posts, with no change in community quality; two-speed movement as entry-driven creative destruction rather than incumbents' own volatility.

## 6. What a PNAS-class paper of this type needs and this one lacks

An out-of-sample test of the central claim (the sealed bridge, protocol §2z-cp). Dependence-aware uncertainty: cluster bootstrap by community, block over time, pooled across starts, for every headline number including the "tripled". A power statement: which alternative values of R̃/c, or which rival worlds, the design can reject, so the band [0.75, 1.33] is justified rather than asserted. A measurement-artifact section: capture delay and deletion by community size and month, flagged weeks, vote fuzzing. One reconciled registration record (git hashes of each `DESIGN.md`, the scoreboard, and failures) in the SI. The theory stated with its conditions, the discrete correction at the head, and the non-additivity of R. The hybrid-Atlas positioning and the Gomez decomposition cited. A data-availability statement that acknowledges Pushshift's 2023 access change and CrowdTangle's discontinuation, with derived rank-level panels released. The decomposition figure on all four ladders. Codex's reviews R5/R6 and V5 closed (`coordination/QUEUE.md` rows 21–23).
