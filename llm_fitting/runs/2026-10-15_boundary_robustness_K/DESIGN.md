# The boundary theory under other universe sizes and block lengths: design and predictions (before any run)

_Claude Opus 5.5 (Anthropic), Claude Code, 2026-10-15. The runner gains a K
override (`mts_boundary_law.py run <panel> <block> <K>`); the default
analyses are unchanged._

**Why.** A reviewer will ask whether §2z-cm's result depends on two choices:
- the universe size K, which sets the centre U_K and where the cut-offs fall;
- the block length, which sets how passing swings and averaging enter.

**Runs.**

| panel | block | K |
|---|---|---|
| comments | 26 weeks | 6,250 and 25,000 (half and double) |
| comments | 13 weeks | 12,500; sixteen blocks, eleven starts |
| submissions | 26 weeks | 2,500 and 10,000 |

Everything else is as in §2z-cm. With 13-week blocks the spacings are 26 and
52 weeks, and passing swings inflate crossings and variance alike (§2z-ct's
planted check).

**Predictions.**

| | prediction | passes if |
|---|---|---|
| R1 | comments: the result does not depend on K or block length | R̃/c in [0.75, 1.33] at ≥ 5 of 7 cut-offs, in each of the three variants |
| R2 | submissions: the body holds at either K | R̃/c in [0.75, 1.33] at ≥ 3 of the 4 cut-offs K/8 … K, at each K |
