# External Technical Review: Common Rank-Diffusion Model, Stages 1–4

Read-only review. I read all 1431 lines of `llm_fitting/common_rankdiff.py`, the tests, REPORT and DESIGN, the Stage 3 benchmark and cache builder, all four saved JSONs, MODEL_STATUS §2z-dw and §2z-dx, the GATE_LEDGER tail, PROGRAM_STATE, the takeover plan, and the frozen Gate v1 definitions in `rankdiff_kalman.py`. No data file was opened and no sealed path was touched. Nothing in the repository was modified. A copy of this review is saved outside the repository at `~/.claude/plans/external-technical-review-nifty-planet.md`.

**Verdict in one paragraph.** Stage 2 is a real engineering advance: eleven moment-identified scalars, a correct calendar-aligned filter, a sound structure-function fit with Spec-B as a lower bound, and an honest record with pre-registered predictions scored as partial. It is not yet shown to be better than Model A on the adoption rule, and on one panel its point forecasts lose both to persistence and to Stage 1's one-parameter local level. Stage 3's headline wins are weaker than the report says. The surge half-life sits at the top of its grid on all four panels. Two of the three surge scalars sit on hard clips on three of four panels. The Instagram "σ_d = 0" is a code branch for short panels, not a measurement. The gapped-persistence "win" is close to a tautology because σ_d is identified from the same gapped windows. The rank-scaling of surges was introduced after a Goal 1 score was seen. The platform atlas is confounded with panel length. The model is not ready to freeze.

## 1. Audit of progress and soundness

**1.1 Standing relative to Model A and the three goals.**

- **Goal 3 (parsimony):** genuinely better than Model A's several hundred knot values. But the Stage 3 count of 14–15 free scalars is overstated. The saved parameters show boundary estimates everywhere except Facebook:

  The clips are at `common_rankdiff.py:477` (μ) and `:481`, `:485` (λ); the grid is `:46`. Hidden constants that act as degrees of freedom: 1.35 (`:477`), the 0.35 variance budget (`:495`), the 0.85 cap (`:608`), the 0.025 and 0.02 activation thresholds (`:472`), the 5μ cap (`:963`, `:1069`), hl_d = 156 (`:47`), and PHI_GRID.
- **Goal 2 (movement):** beats `no_change` and `rank_bin` on logR CRPS, but two comparisons a referee will demand are missing from REPORT §4B. First, relative-level MAE against persistence. On submissions, Stage 2 loses at every horizon and Stage 3 loses at h ≥ 13. Share-shift MAE on submissions is two to three times worse than persistence at every horizon under Stage 2. - **Goal 1 (structure):** turnover and boundary ratio are close, but partly by construction. The drift refinement at `:280-297` targets zero ladder trend, and the crossing identity holds for any diffusion. The heads' fall is not reproduced on either Reddit ladder (see §2). On asymmetry, Facebook Stage 3 gives D = +0.078 [0.050, 0.095] while the data's cluster-bootstrap CI is [−0.009, +0.071] (stage1 JSON). The intervals do not overlap. That is a rejection, not "matches sign".
- **Versus Model A on the gate:** not comparable yet. REPORT §4C's table placing 0.181 beside movinghome_vp's 0.123 is not a comparison. The footnote admits it; the executive summary does not.

**1.2 Component audit.**

1. **Split-half s_vol** (`:173-209`). Correct idea, and the zero-s recovery is a real fix over Codex V2. But residualization is a quadratic in z only (`:205`). Any z-structure beyond quadratic, level dependence through β, absence patterns, or heterogeneous loadings on the common per-period factor load into "s". The recovery test uses the model's own DGP and cannot detect this. A constant of 0.69–0.76 across platforms whose σ_obs differs threefold is consistent with a mechanical origin, such as count-noise heterogeneity at fixed rank.
2. **Empirical-Bayes v̂** (`:1163-1178`). Shrinkage weight s²/(s² + 3/n_d) uses a hand "heavy-tail factor 1.5". Centering and renormalization are fine. With gamma_L = 0 the amplitude scales ξ and ε only, consistent with the model.
3. **Profile NNLS and Spec-B lower bound** (`:581-624`). Sound. Columns [2, 2(1−φ^h), h] are nearly collinear at long h, so the SSE surface is flat in φ. The grid points 0.84, 0.89, 0.93 are adjacent, and φ moves between Stage 2 and Stage 3 on three of four panels, traded against V_s and σ_d. "Universal φ ∈ [0.84, 0.93]" is three grid points. The linear-in-h lasting term ignores the mean reversion that the balancing drift induces, acceptable at h ≤ 52.
4. **Stage 3 subtraction** (`:754-766`). The logic is right. The 0.85 cap and 0.35 budget are undocumented. On submissions the subtraction drives σ_L0 from 0.098 to 0.043 and β to −0.031. Long-horizon lasting dispersion is then carried by the drift: σ_d√f_vel is 0.39 log units at 52 weeks and 1.42 at 212 weeks. That is what fans the head out.
5. **Surge identification** (`:416-507`). I verified the κ3 identity: κ3(Δ_h s) = κ3(J)·3ρ^h(1−ρ^h)/(1−ρ³) with κ3(J) = λμ³(6−6λ+2λ²). Correct. Four problems. (a) The profile 3ρ^h(1−ρ^h) is monotone over hs ≤ 26 for every hl ≥ 20, so hl ∈ {26, 52, 104} are not separable with hs = {4, 8, 13, 20, 26}; hl_s = 26 everywhere is the grid ceiling, not a universal 26-week decay. (b) The statistic E[Z³|Z|] − 4E|Z|E[Z³] does vanish for symmetric noise, but its scale depends on the symmetric component's distribution, so the divisor 1.35 cannot be universal, and three panels land on the clip. (c) The simulator caps the exponential at 5μ while the identities assume no cap. I computed the capped moments: E[E²] = 1.918 against 2, E[E³] = 5.25 against 6. The simulated surge variance is 7% low and its third cumulant 25% low relative to what the estimator assumed. (d) The innovation pool is built from one-week changes that contain the surges (`:854-866`), is drawn for L, ξ and ε (`:1021`, `:1065-1066`), and then surges are added again (`:1069`). Skew is double-counted.
6. **Rank scaling by b_ξ** (`:403-413`). Zero extra scalars, but it was introduced after v1's uniform surge inflated Facebook top-10 turnover to 0.65 (REPORT §5 item 4; MODEL_STATUS failed variant 4). That is a structural choice made on a Goal 1 score, which contradicts the zero-tuning claim. Stage 3 v2 still gives 0.50 [0.45, 0.60] against 0.35. The maximum head jump is 6·s_sc ≈ 3.5 log units on Facebook, and Stage 3 max_X q95 is 20.4 against an observed 16.85. "Zero expm1 blowups" is overstated. Blowups are reduced from §2z-du's +10 log units, not eliminated.
7. **σ_d** (`:510-578`). The gapped-covariance algebra is correct. But `:527-532` returns σ_d = 0 with reason `short_panel` whenever T < 75, and Instagram has T = 52. The REPORT's "turns off automatically on broadcast feeds" is, for Instagram, a code path. Facebook (T = 86) uses W, H, g = 4, 26, 4. A 156-week half-life is unidentifiable from 26-week gapped windows in 86 weeks; the zero follows from the already-negative empirical gapped ρ. Worse, for Reddit the identifying windows (13/52/13) are the Goal 1 evaluator's gapped-persistence windows (`stage3_benchmark.py:44-45`, `:197-211`). The "out-of-moment" gapped ρ is the Spearman version of the covariance that identified σ_d. With ρ_d^117 = 0.59 over the window span, this is the fixed-growth-differences form of §2z-dq, which §2z-ds rejected on its own calibrated objective. The REPORT does not engage with that record.
8. **Drift refinement and deconvolution** (`:245-342`). Both principled. The refinement runs 52 weeks from the first-block ladder with entity drift active (`:1023-1027`), so it balances the mean drift but cannot stop 212 weeks of near-permanent heterogeneous drift from spreading the head. The "zero-parameter" deconvolution fits N initial values; legitimate as an initial condition, but call it what it is.
9. **Kalman filters** (`:1111-1323`). The two-state algebra is correct and the h = 1 alignment test is right. At first observation P12 should be −V_ξ·v̂, not 0 (`:1215`); it decays fast. The four-state filter treats a compound-Poisson surge as Gaussian, so the posterior on s is poorly calibrated. The forecast clamp (`:1390-1394`) moves any negative clamped surge mass into L permanently, so a deficit that should revert toward s_mean becomes lasting. s_mean = λμ/(1−ρ) is 0.08 on Facebook, 0.11 on comments and 0.57 on Instagram, so the clamp bites hardest on Instagram, consistent with Stage 3 being slightly worse there at h = 1 and 4.
10. **Leakage and harness.** The universe is top-4K by full-panel permanent rank (`build_lean_cache.py:166-175`, `:227-237`). This matches the registered convention but is future-informed in rolling-origin tests and should be stated. Goal 1 is in-sample. The cache lives in a session-specific path outside the repository (`stage3_benchmark.py:38`), so another agent cannot reproduce the runs without rebuilding it.
11. **Tests.** Nine tests with loose tolerances (φ ±0.15, σ_L0 25%, σ_d 35%; recovered σ_d is 28% low). No test plants hl_s > 26, no test uses a misspecified z-profile for s_vol, no filter test against a reference implementation, no test of the gate harness against the frozen one.
12. **Pre-registration drift.** DESIGN §2 registered α_skew identified from the run ratio, a three-state filter and hl grid {8, 13, 26}. The implementation has no estimated skew, a four-state filter and grid {8, 13, 20, 26}. Not disclosed. P3.2's miss on run ratios is partly because the registered component was never built.

**1.3 Atlas credibility.** Likely genuine: crossing-dominated departures; positive rank gradients b_ξ and b_obs; the σ_obs gradient Instagram > Facebook > Reddit; the supply-versus-engagement split in P1.4, which is a data decomposition rather than a model fit. Likely artifacts: hl_s = 26 (grid ceiling); the φ band (grid granularity on a flat profile); σ_d = 0 on feeds (the T branch, and Facebook underpowered); s_vol constancy (needs a count-noise placebo); σ_d identical on the two Reddit ladders (same communities, shared shocks). A matched-length test is required before any feed-versus-community claim.

## 2. Diagnosis of the four gaps

1. **Submissions head share and Gate v1.** Head share is measured in the last block (`stage3_benchmark.py:121-124`). The empirical span above rank K fell by 0.78 log units at rank 78 and 2.03 at rank 1 over the panel. Stage 2 holds the span (−0.00 at rank 78) and Stage 3 raises it (+0.31 [0.18, 0.41] at rank 78; +1.57 at rank 1). The model reproduces the start-of-panel head and is compared with the end-of-panel data. The root cause is the heads' fall recorded in §2z-do and §2z-dq, not β. Stage 3 is worse because 156-week drift spreads the head by 1.4 log units SD over 212 weeks. For the gate, the empirical rank-1 weekly change is 0.057 against 0.031 and 0.033 simulated, coll1 and coll5 sit near the 0.02 floor so relative error explodes, and the point forecast is worse than persistence on this panel.
2. **Run ratios, leverage, submissions D.** Symmetric d_i plus positive surges cannot produce leverage above 1, which requires larger variance after declines. The registered skew component was dropped. The capped exponential cuts simulated κ3 by a quarter. Run ratios rest on seven transitions of 26-week blocks, so power is low either way.
3. **Stage 3 regressions.** Facebook top-10 turnover and boundary 1.134 follow from surge jumps of up to 3.5 log units among top pages spaced about 0.3 apart, and a 26-week mean-reverting component at a 13-week block span breaks the diffusion identity. The report lists only one share-correlation drop, but submissions share_corr falls at all five horizons (0.33→0.18, 0.27→0.07, 0.29→0.17, 0.57→0.36, 0.68→0.53) and comments at four of five. Arithmetic-share ensembles with rare multi-log-unit jumps are extremely skewed, and the s_mean centering lowers every non-surging entity. Comments h = 52 CRPS worsens because drift extrapolated 52 weeks adds dispersion that was not there. Instagram coverage 0.81 is the censoring floor plus the clamp.
4. **Missing evaluations.** The frozen Gate v1; local_level and MAE-versus-persistence rows; the out-of-period 2019–20 → 2021–22 test with head share and span by block; matched-length Reddit at 86 and 52 weeks; data-side cluster-bootstrap bands set against the 20-seed bands; PIT or coverage by horizon; decile persistence curves; recovery with hl_s planted at 52 and 104.

## 3. Next rounds, what not to do, and readiness

**High priority.**
- **H1 Surge identification off the boundaries** (Goals 1 and 3). Extend the hl grid to {8,…,104} and hs to include 39 and 52 on Reddit. Identify μ from κ5/κ3, since odd cumulants of symmetric noise vanish exactly and κ5(J)/κ3(J) = 20μ² to first order in λ, or from upper-versus-lower tail-quantile asymmetry at several quantiles. Remove or moment-correct the 5μ cap. Symmetrize or surge-strip the innovation pool when asymmetry is on. Falsify if hl_s hits the new ceiling or μ differs across h by more than its bootstrap SD. Then the compound-Poisson-exponential surge is rejected and surge and drift should collapse into one skewed persistent component.
- **H2 The heads' fall** (Goal 1). First a zero-model-change diagnostic: head share and span by block, data against 20 seeds. Then the out-of-period test: estimate on 2019–20, simulate 2021–22 from the filtered state, compare end-2022 head share and span. Falsify if the simulated head share stays above the data's band. A stationary balancing drift then cannot carry the structure claim, and "stable ladder" must be restated as held by construction or the incumbents' slide of §2z-bu must be modeled.
- **H3 Goal 2 against local level.** Add local_level and MAE-versus-persistence rows to every table. Diagnose the short-horizon loss on submissions, Facebook and Instagram (P12 initialization, EB weight, ξ-versus-L misattribution). Falsify if the filter with its own (φ, V_ξ, σ_obs, σ_L) cannot beat a one-α EWMA on MAE on at least three panels. The passing-versus-lasting split is then misestimated.
- **H4 Frozen Gate v1** at the frozen calendar, five splits by twenty reps, all four panels, logged as candidate rows. Non-inferiority against Model A's reference rows. AGENTS.md requires this for any adoption.
- **H5 Matched length.** Re-estimate Stage 3 on Reddit cut to 86 weeks from 2020-12-28 (R19's cut) and to 52 weeks. Register beforehand that σ_d → 0 on the cuts through the T branch. The feed-versus-community divide is falsified if 86-week Reddit σ_d is zero or Facebook-like.

**Medium priority.**
- **M1 Consistency fixes** with no new scalars: cap versus identity, pool skew, P12, and the s clamp (reflect, or draw from a truncated-normal posterior, rather than absorbing into L).
- **M2 s_vol placebo.** Estimate on the N and E channels separately, on a Poisson-thinned surrogate with s = 0, and on a planted panel with a non-quadratic z-profile. Universality is falsified if the surrogate returns about 0.7.
- **M3 Data-side bands.** A cluster-bootstrap CI on every Goal 1 statistic beside the 20-seed band; a statistic matches only if the intervals overlap.
- **M4 Re-register Stage 3** (α_skew dropped, grid changed) before further work.

**Low priority.** Decile persistence curves, PIT histograms, recovery and power for hl_s ∈ {52, 104} and for σ_d at Facebook's length.

**Do not** fit more surge or drift variants to the same four panels, tune the surge rank profile further, add a skew parameter before H1, spend more on Facebook beyond H2 and H3 (86 weeks cannot identify 156-week drift or 26-week-plus surges), or run referee rounds.

**Readiness verdict: not ready to freeze.** The minimum bar before a confirmation protocol:

1. H4 run and logged, non-inferior to Model A, or an owner decision recorded on other grounds.
2. No Stage 3 scalar on a clip or grid edge on any development panel, or those constants declared fixed and the scalar count restated.
3. Goal 2 beats local_level on logR CRPS and does not lose to persistence on MAE on at least three panels, with coverage in [0.85, 0.95].
4. The H2 out-of-period test run and reported.
5. H5 run, so the platform claim is either an artifact or survives.
6. REPORT and §2z-dx corrected (Instagram σ_d branch, max_X, share MAE, local_level, design drift) and committed with the ledgers. The §2z-dw entry and the takeover document are also still uncommitted after two weeks, which puts the record at risk.
7. A blind re-implementation by Codex of the three Stage 3 estimators from the formulas alone, agreeing within Monte Carlo error.

Then freeze Stage 2, and Stage 3 only if item 2 holds, register T1–T5 with bands from the development cluster bootstrap, and only then open held-out data.
