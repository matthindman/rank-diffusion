## A correction found while answering N3

R18-C's summary showed the β 0.019 matched control's head share with a 5–95% band of [0.02, 0.33]. Per-run values (`r18_world__facebook_a__*__s*.json`): two runs at 0.010 and 0.023, so the mean is 0.253 and the median 0.281. Every other Facebook arm except the 0.097 matched control has one such run (0.000–0.019); the constant-slope arm at 0.019 has another at 0.10.

A post hoc diagnostic (`r18c_headshare_diag.py`, rebuilding controlb0.019 seed 0 exactly) finds the cause. In that run the unit at block rank 87 has one simulated week at 27.2 log units, about ten above the leader's 17. That week holds 96% of the top K's level sum.

The cause is the heavy-tailed passing swing: `fb_stats` computes the head share from expm1(X) summed over the last block. Every other statistic is a rank or log statistic, and in the degenerate runs all of them are ordinary.

Consequences:
- The head share is now reported as the median over runs. `r18_facebook._band` gains a `median` field, and `r18_summary.json` was regenerated: identical except for the added medians.
- R18-B2 was met as registered, on the mean (0.25). By the median (0.28) it misses ±0.05 by 0.002. The reading "the size law accounts for the head's share" (RESULTS R18-B; `RESPONSE_opus_iter1.md`, item 1; v9.2's Results 4) is withdrawn. The size law lowers the matched control's median head share only from 0.30 to 0.28.
- Results 4 now says the model "gives the head too large a share (0.28 against 0.23, median of runs)".

The record files are append-only, so RESULTS R18-B and the iteration-1 response are corrected by RESULTS R18-C and by this file, not edited.
