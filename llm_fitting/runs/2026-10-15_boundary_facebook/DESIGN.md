# The boundary theory on a second platform, Facebook pages (Era A): design and predictions (before any run)

_Claude Opus 5.5 (Anthropic), Claude Code, 2026-10-15. Committed with the
generalised runner (`mts_boundary_law.py run facebook_a 13`) and a
13-week-block planted test before the run._

## Why

The boundary theory has been tested only on Reddit (§2z-cm, §2z-co). A
reviewer's first question will be whether it holds on another platform.
`facebook_a`, Facebook pages' interactions in Era A, is DEVELOPMENT data
(86 weeks, K = 3,500, the panel step 1 used, §2z-bu). It is the only other
platform open to us.

## Method

As §2z-cm, with 13-week blocks:
- six blocks; one start (block 1, cohort fixed on block 0);
- crossings from spacings of 26 and 52 weeks;
- variance by the route over blocks 1, 3, 5 (26-week spacing);
- strata and cut-offs K/64 … K as before.

**Planted check, three seeds, 13-week blocks.**
- *Plain walks:* the crossings match σ²/2S within a third at K/4 … K. This
  is the committed test.
- *Passing swings (variance 0.3, half-life 10 or 26 weeks):* the crossings
  and the variance route are both inflated, by 1.1–1.4 against the truth,
  because swings do not fade within 26 weeks. The tested ratio R̃/c stays
  0.92–1.23 at K/8 … K.
- So the test is meaningful on a panel with large swings, but the absolute
  rates are not.

**Panel limits.**
- The boundary strata at K/64 and K/32 hold about 27–55 pages: noise, as on
  submissions.
- Facebook's passing swings are larger than Reddit's (§2z-bu, §2z-by), which
  is the case the planted swings cover.

## Predictions

| | prediction | passes if |
|---|---|---|
| F1 | crossings follow σ²/(2S) on Facebook pages | R̃/c in [0.75, 1.33] at ≥ 4 of the 5 cut-offs K/16 … K |
| F2 | once one-time constants are removed, the slide of whoever holds the top k equals the cohort's | as §2z-cm B2, at ≥ 5 of 7 cut-offs |

**What it means.**
- *F1 pass.* The law's physical claim holds on a second platform, with a
  different metric (page interactions), population (pages) and recommender
  regime.
- *F1 fail with large swings.* An instrument limit to report, not a
  refutation, unless the planted swing worlds already cover the panel's swing
  sizes.
