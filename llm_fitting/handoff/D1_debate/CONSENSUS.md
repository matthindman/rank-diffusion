# Consensus on candidate 2 — for the owner

_Drafted by Claude (Opus 5.5) on 2026-10-12 from the wording Codex proposed
in `CODEX_2.md`, after two independent first positions and two rounds.
Codex's sign-off is `CODEX_SIGNOFF.md`. This is a recommendation. It adopts
nothing, and agreement between two models is not evidence: every empirical
statement below names its record entry or the measurement that would test
it. No model code, fit, simulation or gate evaluation was run for it._

## The owner's four questions

**1. Under "pull toward the ladder at the current rank", can the rank
structure emerge from micro-level behaviour?** No. The proposal supplies
the shape of the ladder and builds movement to restore it. Your reading is
correct for that proposal. It is not correct of rank-dependent models in
general: a law in which growth depends on an endpoint's state, with no
ladder written into it, can generate one.

**2. Is the model as it stands any different?** Not on this point. It gives
each endpoint a home taken from the measured ladder. Neither model explains
why the hierarchy has its shape. The binding claim already says so
(conditional reproduction and maintenance; MODEL_STATUS §1).

**3. For a paper that aims at causes and not at predictions built into its
assumptions, what should the lasting layer be?** Nothing is identified yet,
and choosing between two supplied targets would not get you there. Three
levels of claim have to be kept apart:

- *conditional dynamics*: given a ladder, describe movement. The binding
  model claim is here now, and that is a legitimate place to be;
- *distributional explanation*: estimate movement and turnover without
  using the ladder, freeze them, and predict structure that was held out;
- *social mechanism*: show which audience, supply or platform process
  changes growth and concentration, with a design that separates
  explanations. These panels may not contain such a design.

The second level is the one that answers your concern, and whether it is
reachable with these data is not known. Candidate 2 would have sat at the
first level while looking like the second.

**4. What should happen next?** See "Recommendation".

## Recommendation

1. Withdraw candidate 2 as the proposed replacement now and spend no gate
   evaluation on it. It is unselected, not refuted: as a conditional
   description its restoring law could still be tested.
2. Keep the present model as the conditional benchmark. Its fitted lasting
   dynamics are challenged at horizons up to about a year and a half
   (§2z-aw, §2z-ay, strengthened by planted checks in §2z-bc and §2z-bd),
   and its fitted reversion rates are poorly recovered on simulated data
   (§2z-bc). That does not rule out weak long-run reversion or durable
   identity effects that act other than through a fixed level.
3. Next, a design written and reviewed before anything runs, for a
   diagnostic batch on development data:
   - whether the lasting level moves back toward the endpoint's own
     earlier level, toward a train-only ladder at its current rank, or
     toward neither;
   - turnover at nested cutoffs of the recorded list, with entries from
     the buffer, re-entries and never-before-seen endpoints kept apart;
   - whether growth depends on state, with size and rank treated as one
     family unless something exogenous separates them.
   Each contrast is validated against planted rivals, with nuisance
   uncertainty, before it is read on real data. The lasting level used in
   a test must not come from the filter whose assumptions are being
   tested. Ending with "the data cannot tell these apart" is an acceptable
   result.
4. Only if usable primitives come out of that: estimate and freeze them
   without tuning to the ladder to be evaluated, then test held-out ladder
   and movement predictions together, stating everything the model was
   allowed to condition on, including rank labels and the universe. A
   convergence experiment from altered initial states belongs here and
   only for a model with no target in its parameters, with convergence
   time reported against the length of the panel.

## What would change this

- A restoring response toward an earlier identity anchor that survives
  noise and seasonal controls, with movement predictions that transport:
  a home model is supported (not necessarily the present one).
- A stable restoring response toward a train-only ladder that beats the
  alternatives on temporal predictions: candidate 2 returns as a
  conditional model, counted honestly, including the supplied ladder and
  every retained profile.
- Independently measured dynamics that predict unused structure and
  movement: a derived-ladder model is supported.
- None of the above can be told apart: the limited claim stays and that
  result is reported.

Any candidate still faces the unchanged adoption rule and your decision.

## What is not settled

- Whether a random walk and current-rank attraction can be told apart
  from how endpoints move. The declared contrast in V2 could not (§2z-bc,
  prediction P11). The full pairs table labels 42 to 68 of 650 to 780
  statistics as separated for the strong pair on each profile,
  concentrated at the head and in the shell, with no family-wide error
  control. The test is the first item of the diagnostic batch.
- What generates the hierarchy. This debate settled what a supplied target
  assumes. It cannot settle what produces the ladder.

## Flags for you, changing nothing

- The outline's sentence that the random-growth tradition "is silent on
  who occupies the rungs" is too sweeping.
- "Durable concentration without durable incumbents" has to be a tested
  and scoped result. It is not yet one.
- A fitted reversion rate, an unvalidated gap-closure ratio, or the
  failure of selected planted contrasts should not be used as a mechanism
  classifier.
- A sentence in the repository's literature note, that the expected top
  share is unbounded under heavy-tailed shocks, is wrong (a share lies
  between zero and one) and should not be carried into the paper.

## What we ask you to decide

Whether the design for the diagnostic batch in item 3 should be written.
Nothing runs before you have seen that design.
