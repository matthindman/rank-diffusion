### Answers to the brief

**Walks and restoring signs.** The plain fixed walk gives a negative
position association in 1/20 [0.009,0.236] seeds on each Reddit profile;
the joint sign occurs in comments 0/20 [0.000,0.161] and submissions
1/20 [0.009,0.236]. Adding persistent passing noise raises the joint rates
to 20/20 and 19/20 as above, although neither process has a personal home.
B3 rejects common bin behavior for neither of those walks on either
Reddit profile (0/20 [0.000,0.161] in each case). This does not establish
equivalent bin means: many B3 results are INCONCLUSIVE. The complete sign,
eligibility and B3 tables include every rival, not selected counterexamples.

**Home detection and short panels.** The classifier recovers the home
class 20/20 on all profiles. That observation is sharply limited: the
Facebook design cannot compute the primary earlier-level carrier at all;
all 20 comments home primary pairs fail exact-rank certification, so the
classifier can use their predeclared missing-feature indicators. Submissions
home has the joint restoring sign in 20/20, but persistent-noise walks
nearly duplicate that sign. Moreover, the home arm differs in marginals
from every non-control rival on comments, and from seven of the eight on
Facebook (replacement is the exception). High classification there is
not clean recovery of home reversion at matched marginals. Submissions
home matches all eight non-control rivals under the declared comparison,
but that finite-menu separation still does not make the carrier sign unique.

**Replacement and position drift.** The point-classification distinction
is available on both Reddit profiles, with the uncertainty and marginal
qualifications in P4. Facebook rank-drift recovery is only 12/20
[0.387,0.781], versus replacement 20/20 and position-OU 20/20. The complete
rival-level confusion matrix, including false labels within pooled classes,
is `confusion.csv.gz`; `classifications.csv.gz` records missing fractions.
Across all 45 pairs per profile, matched/mismatched/control counts are
19/17/9 (Facebook), 27/9/9 (comments), and 30/6/9 (submissions).

**Passing swings survive block averaging.** For the fixed walk, median
nuisance occupant loss per endpoint-week at K/4 is 0.001141
[0.0006178,0.001349] on Facebook, 0.0002862 [0.0001135,0.0004809]
on comments, and 0.00007979 [-0.0001855,0.0002849] on submissions.
With persistent passing noise the corresponding medians are 0.003024
[0.002443,0.003791], 0.0009124 [0.0006692,0.001056], and 0.0004981
[0.0002626,0.0007832]. These are oracle additive decompositions using
the same observed occupant and centre sets, not observable identification
of the passing layer. Fractions can exceed one when components offset.
The predictable nuisance share does not generally equal this loss; its
full seed bands and eligibility counts are provided. Coverage cannot
certify that it separates the components because the target is too noisy.

**Finite-horizon gain.** All 52-week rival/boundary medians and seed bands
are tabulated, retaining the uncertified comments-home case as unavailable.
Replacement has substantial discrepancies at the larger boundaries;
fixed-walk medians range from 0.972 to 1.058 over comments boundaries
and 0.978 to 1.012 over submissions boundaries. These boundary ranges are
descriptive; uncertainty is in the separate seed bands at each boundary.

### Held-out slopes and interval coverage

The full 30-row recovery table is `RECOVERY_MAP.md`. All five future local
slopes had to be within 25% in a seed, and its success rate then needed a
Wilson lower bound of 0.80. No row meets this rule. The observed maximum
success count is comments persistent-noise walk 13/20 [0.433,0.819]; this
is reported as the maximum of the entire fixed grid, not as a selected
successful model. The frozen home arm is 0/20 [0.000,0.161] on every
profile. Of 3,000 individual boundary forecasts, 2,405 are valid and 595

The failures comprise 475 nonpositive/unavailable-input forecasts and

Signed observed and latent errors are saved for every eligible boundary,
with all-five maximum-error bands in COMPLETION_TABLES.

The protocol screen passes 0/200 Facebook, 75/200 comments and 22/200
submissions evaluation cases (descriptive counts across ten rivals).

walk succeeds in 10/16 [0.386,0.815]. Every conditional rival/profile rate
is in `rates.csv.gz`; zero eligible cases remain NA, not zero success.

Coverage uses the independent calibration-seed bin-population target,
with realised evaluation centre/count/R values plugged in. There are
